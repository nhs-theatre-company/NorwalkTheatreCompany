About the Show

Macbeth tells the story of a Scottish general who slowly descends into madness and paranoia, too focused on keeping power, all because of a prophecy.

Audition Requirements

Audition Form

Complete the audition/interview form BEFORE your scheduled audition time slot. It is on google classroom.

Macbeth Monologue

Any actor can audition for any role, regardless of gender. You must present one monologue from Macbeth (even not in the packet).

Contemporary Monologue

You may preform a second contemporary monologue of your choice memorized.
