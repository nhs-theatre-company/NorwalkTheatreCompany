Our Recent Productions

Both in our musicals and plays we try our hardest to immerse the audience in the story, bringing a different but impactful community each time.

Spring Musical

Chicago

This show trained our actors heavy with dance, showing how corruption could let people get away with so much, even murder. This was our first time having our pit on the stage, making it completely different than past musicals.

Winter Play

All in the Timing

This show was all about repetition. It challenged our actors to know lines by the word, to not mess anything up. We even won an award for it being an amazing one-act play!

Fall Play

Fools

Improv work! Although we were true to the script, we were able to work on improvising our humor and having the time of our lives on the MTC stage!
