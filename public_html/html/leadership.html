BEHIND THE SCENES

The People Who Make It Possible

Each production brings together different sections of preforming arts to make each production amazing. Whether it be the band, the performers or the crew, each person has to step out of their field to work together, combining students and adults alike.

THEATRE

Charley McAfee

Mac has been dedicated to theatre for essentially his whole life, and joined our company in 2021! He works in classes and after school, and his main priority is educating, not just producing.

CHOIR

Steven Oliveri

Oliveri is amazingly educated in voice, and will always make sure to highlight the talents of our amazing singers. Even outside of our theatre, he runs our entire choir!

BAND

Chris Rivera

Known for his strict program, Rivera is a big staple in making our music in the musicals POP! He has lead our band to have successful seasons year after year, and will continue to do so!
