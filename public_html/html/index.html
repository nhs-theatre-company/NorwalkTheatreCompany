Our Company

Norwalk Theatre Co. is a student-driven theatre program at Norwalk High School. We produce multiple shows each year, including a fall and winter play, along with a spring musical that brings together performers, designers, and crew alike. We work as hard as we can to produce top-quality shows that are unlike anything you've seen before.

Whether you're on stage, behind the scenes, or in the audience, there's a place for you here.

Shakespeare’s Macbeth is a dark and powerful tragedy that explores ambition, fate, and the consequences of unchecked power. Our fall production will bring this classic story to life through dynamic performances, striking visuals, and an immersive theatrical experience.
